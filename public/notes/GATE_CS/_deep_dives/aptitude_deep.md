## 1. Verbal Ability

## 2. Quantitative Aptitude — Full Formula Bank

### Percentages
- $x\%$ of $y = \frac{xy}{100}$
- Successive change: $a\% + b\% + \frac{ab}{100}\%$

### Profit & Loss
- $\text{SP} = \text{CP}(1 + \frac{\text{profit\%}}{100})$
- Discount: $\text{SP} = \text{MP}(1 - \frac{\text{discount\%}}{100})$

### Time & Work
- Work $= \text{Rate} \times \text{Time}$
- If A does work in $x$ days, rate $= \frac{1}{x}$.

### Pipes & Cisterns
- Inlet adds, outlet removes. Net rate $= \text{inlet} - \text{outlet}$.

### Averages
- $\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}$

### Mixtures & Alligation
- Alligation rule: $\frac{\text{Cheaper quantity}}{\text{Dearer quantity}} = \frac{\text{D} - \text{M}}{\text{M} - \text{C}}$

## 4. Logical Reasoning

### Syllogisms
- Use Venn diagrams.
- "All A are B" → A inside B.
- "Some A are B" → overlapping circles.

#### 4.1 Syllogism Venn Diagram Table

| Statement | Venn diagram | Valid conclusion |
|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| All A are B | A ⊂ B | Some B are A |
| No A are B | A and B disjoint | No B are A |
| Some A are B | A ∩ B ≠ ∅ | Some B are A |
| Some A are not B | A has part outside B | — |

#### 4.2 Worked Example — Syllogism (Table)

**Premises:** All cats are animals. Some animals are pets.

| Conclusion | Valid? | Reason |
|------------|--------|--------|
| Some cats are pets | ❌ | The "some animals" that are pets may not include cats |
| All cats are pets | ❌ | Contradicts premise |
| Some animals are cats | ✅ | Follows from "all cats are animals" |
| No cats are pets | ❌ | Not guaranteed |

> 💡 **GATE trap:** "Some A are B" does NOT imply "Some A are not B" — they can overlap fully. Always draw the Venn diagram before concluding.

## 5. Extended PYQs

**Q6 (GATE 2016, 1 mark):** "The chairman ___ the members for their cooperation." (A) thanked (B) thanked to (C) thanked with (D) thanked for
- **Answer: (A)** — "thanked" takes a direct object.

**Q7 (GATE 2015, 2 marks):** A person walks 10 m north, then 10 m east, then 10 m south. Distance from start:
- **Answer:** 10 m east.

**Q8 (GATE 2014, 1 mark):** If $2^x = 8$, then $x$ is:
- **Answer:** 3.
